Meta checks two things before it verifies a business: that the company legally exists under its name, and that the address or phone number belongs to that company. Please send a soft copy of each document below. The first two are required. The rest are needed if Meta rejects one or asks for more.

How each file must look

  • A colour scan, or a sharp photo taken flat in good light.
  • The whole page, all four corners visible. Nothing cropped.
  • Not edited or written on after it was issued.
  • Current. For bills and bank statements, aim for the last three months.
  • PDF, JPG or PNG, under 10 MB each.
  • One document per file, named plainly: 1 CAC certificate.pdf.

Meta does not accept

  • Invoices, receipts or purchase orders.
  • Forms the company filled in itself.
  • Printouts of a website.
  • A personal ID card, passport or driver's licence.
  • Expired or altered documents.
  • A bill that shows a phone number but no company name.
