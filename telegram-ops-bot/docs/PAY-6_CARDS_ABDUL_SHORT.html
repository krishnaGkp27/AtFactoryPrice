Payments — the short guide

Abdul — four parts, seven steps: what you tap and what arrives on your phone.
What you will do

1. Register your bank account (once). 2. Ask for a payment. 3. Wait: two admins approve, then finance pays. 4. See your requests, and record a payment a customer made.

Who else is involved
Krishnaadmin — approves
Musaadmin — approves, and chooses the company account that pays you
Financethe person who makes the bank transfer

Take a screenshot of the whole card, with its buttons, and send it to Krishna with the step number. Do not tap anything else on that card until he answers.
