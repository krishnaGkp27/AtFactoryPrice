Payments live test — Operations Officer guide

For the two Operations Officers who raise payment requests: each of you does every step of this guide on your own phone, with your own account and your own request.
Date: 09-Sep-2026  ·  Telegram: Black Panther_Bot  ·  About 10 minutes of tapping, then waiting
The one payment everyone is testing

Mr. Abdul (Operations Officer) asks for ₦4,000 into OPAY 7048940378, reason Transport to Idumota, with a photo of the bill.

The Owner signs first, Admin 2 signs second. A payment needs two different admins when two are set up; if the Owner is the only admin, his one approval completes it. The person who asked can never sign his own.

The Office phone (finance) pays by hand in the bank app, taps ✔ Mark Done, then sends the screenshot.

In this guide the Operations Officer is called Mr. Abdul. Read your own name wherever it says Abdul.

Before you start
  • The owner has put you on the Users list as an active employee. You are not an admin — if you were, you could not be a requester in this test.
  • You have sent /start to the bot at least once, so it can message you.
  • Your bank is in the bot's bank list. For OPAY the owner adds it first — if you do not see 🏛 OPAY at step 3, stop and tell him.
  • Have the bill photo on your phone (any receipt or invoice picture will do for the test).
  • Know your account number by heart — the bot asks for it twice and compares.
  • If you already have an approved account in the bot, skip steps 2–5 and start at step 6.
