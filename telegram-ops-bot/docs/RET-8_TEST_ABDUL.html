3Goods with no rate on the sale
Sometimes the sale was saved with no rate. Before, such a return gave the customer NO credit.

Now your confirm card says “⚠️ Thans without a rate: the approving admin is asked for the rate.” You do nothing more. Submit as always.

The first admin types the rate. The second admin checks it. When both approve, your last card shows the credit: “↩️ Credited solder madam 261,000”.

You never see the rate the admin typed. You see only the credit.
