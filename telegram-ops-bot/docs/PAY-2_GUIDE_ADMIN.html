Payments live test — Admin guide

For the two admins — the Owner and Admin 2. Both of you get the same cards; the guide marks what only the first signer or only the second signer sees. Decide between you who signs first; the owner may be either.
Date: 09-Sep-2026  ·  Telegram: Black Panther_Bot  ·  Two taps per request, then waiting
The one payment everyone is testing

Abdul (a worker) asks for ₦4,000 into OPAY 7048940378, reason Transport to Idumota, with a photo of the bill.

The Owner signs first, Admin 2 signs second. A payment needs two different admins when two are set up; if the Owner is the only admin, his one approval completes it. The person who asked can never sign his own.

The Office phone (finance) pays by hand in the bank app, taps ✔ Mark Done, then sends the screenshot.

In this guide the first signer is the Owner and the second is Admin 2. Swap the names if you decide the other way round.

Before you start
  • Both of you have been set up by the owner as admins of the bot. With only one admin there, a single signature would be enough and the test proves nothing.
  • Both of you have sent /start to the bot.
  • The finance seat is set: open 💳 Payments and check the last line reads Finance: Office pays and marks done. If it shows a ⚠️ warning instead, stop — see step 1.
  • Neither of you raises the test request — an admin who asks counts as his own first signature and is left off his own card.
  • The bot's bank list includes OPAY (the owner adds it), or the worker cannot register the example account.
  • Agree who is the first signer. The second signer must wait for the pointer message (step 6) before tapping.

The two exits  ·  run one of each after the first payment
A
❌ Reject — any ONE admin, before approval
Do: on a fresh request (the worker raises one), one admin taps ❌ Reject on the card in your chat with the bot or in the inbox. The bot asks for no reason. For this test, reject before either admin has signed — that keeps the record line below exactly as printed.
You receive: a small grey pop-up Rejecting..., your card's buttons vanish, then “❌ Request … rejected.” The worker gets “❌ Your request R-… has been rejected by admin.” The other admin is not told — even if he gave the first signature — and finance gets nothing (no finance card ever existed). The inbox record reads ❌ Rejected by Owner.
If the reject follows the other admin's first signature, the record names both: ❌ Rejected by Owner + Admin 2 (first signer + rejecter). That is why the test rejects before any signature.
If the bot says something else:
  • ⚠️ Rejection failed: <message> — tell the owner.
