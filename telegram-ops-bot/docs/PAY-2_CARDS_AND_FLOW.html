PAY-2 — Payment request lifecycle: cards and flow

Every card as it prints in Telegram today, with the owner's 09-Sep lifecycle drawn over it — reason line, finance seat, "Paid" reflected back to everyone who approved.

09-Sep-2026 · drawn from a verified read of telegram-ops-bot at that date · worked example: Abdul asks for ₦4,000 into OPAY 7048940378, reason "Transport to Idumota"; admins Ajeet and John; finance = the Office phone.

Dark card / plain row = today (verified in code, file:line given) Yellow line or dashed box new = PROPOSED — does not exist yet Dashed arrow = proposed delivery (a solid arrow into a dashed box = a message that exists today, with proposed wording)
The owner's lifecycle, in one line: admin pool receives the request → approved → a detailed card naming the approvers reaches the finance account (the Railway finance ID) → finance opens the bank app and pays by hand → finance marks it done → "done" is reflected back to everyone who was in the approval queue. Every request now carries a typed reason (3–120 chars, after the amount; chips and an index later; stored in Postgres — specs/PAY-2_PAYMENT_REASON.md).
Known fact to rule on: today the finance card is routed by paymentService.financeHead() = the single Users-sheet row whose department is Finance (if there is none, or more than one, it goes to all env admins with a warning line). The Railway variable FINANCE_IDS (config.access.financeIds, FIN-V1) is used only for read-only gates (balance, container values, Payouts/Incentives, Manage Users summary) and never for the payment card — so the finance ID the owner marked in Railway does not receive it today. See Q1.

4 · Questions for the owner

One line each; the recommended answer comes first.

  1. Q1 — Finance recipient rule. Recommended: the card goes to every id in Railway FINANCE_IDS; if that is empty, the Users 'Finance' department row; if both are empty, all admins with the warning line — and ✔ Mark Done / ✖ Decline are honoured from any of those ids. Alternative: keep the Users sheet as the only source and add Finance to the Office phone's department cell yourself (the two mechanisms are unrelated today).
  2. Q2 — "Reflected back to all". Recommended: the 💸 Paid / ✖ Declined notice goes to the requester + the two admins who signed (Ajeet and John) only; every other admin sees the outcome in the 🛂 inbox record. Alternative: every admin in the pool gets the notice too (more noise on every payment).
  3. Q3 — Proof on the Paid notice. Recommended: yes — when finance attached a transfer screenshot, the Paid notice is sent as that photo with the text as caption (requester and approvers); without proof, plain text. Alternative: text only, proof kept on the row for the record.
  4. Q4 — Inbox record after Done. Recommended: yes — the resolved inbox view for a payment reads the PaymentRequests row and adds one line: 💸 Paid by Office · when · PAY-… / 🏦 With finance to pay / ✖ Declined by Office · reason; and the finance card's buttons are wiped once Done or Declined. Alternative: leave the inbox at "Already approved" and rely on the DMs.
  5. Q5 — Bill on the admin card. Recommended: forward the bill photo/PDF with the admin approval card and give the inbox card a 📄 chip, as sale documents already have; today admins read "Bill: attached" without the file (only finance gets it).
  6. Q6 — Reject flips the row. Recommended: an admin Reject also flips the PaymentRequests row to rejected (today it stays pending_approval forever and My requests keeps saying "waiting"); and Reject asks a one-line reason like Decline does. Alternative: flip only, no reason.
  7. Q7 — Finance reminder and queue. Recommended: an approved-but-unpaid payment re-sends the finance card after PAYMENT_FINANCE_REMINDER_HOURS (Settings, default 4) and the Payments hub gains a "💳 Waiting for me to pay" list for the finance seat (awaitingPayment() exists with no caller); today a missed finance card is never re-sent.
  8. Q8 — Small fixes to ride along. Recommended: escape or send plain the Paid / Declined DMs (a payee or bank name with _ * ` [ silently loses the message today); store approved_by as the pair; show the requester's name, not the raw id, on the finance card; put payments in their own 🛂 inbox category with the dual-admin badge. Say if any of these should wait.
