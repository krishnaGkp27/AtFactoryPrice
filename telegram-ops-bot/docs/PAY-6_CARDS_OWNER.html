Set-upSet up the finance phone
Do this once, before the first payment. The finance phone is the ONE phone that pays: it gets the payment card, taps ✔ Mark Done after the bank transfer, and sends the proof. Whoever approves a payment can never pay it (the four-eyes rule) — so the finance phone must belong to someone who never approves.

Your paying accounts — the company accounts money leaves FROM

For now they are kept by name: BANK — ACCOUNT NAME, for example ZENITH — BLACK PANTHER and GTBANK — BLACK PANTHER. The bot finds them in one of two places:

  • The accounts already listed in 🏦 Manage Banks that are written as BANK — ACCOUNT NAME. A plain bank name (GTBank, Zenith …) is a bank, not a company account, and is never offered.
  • Or a list typed in Settings, key PAYMENT_SOURCE_ACCOUNTS — one account per line. When this cell has anything in it, it is used instead of the Manage Banks list.
How many paying accountsWhat happens on the last ✅ Approve
two or moreThe admin giving the last approval is asked “Pay from which account?” and taps one (Part D).
exactly oneIt is used without asking, and named on every card that follows.
0 in that Settings cellSwitched off: nobody is asked, and the finance card shows only the account paid TO, as before.

Account numbers, and the QR details shown when a chip is tapped, come in a later release (your words: “polished later”). Note: because they live in the Manage Banks list, these two company accounts also appear as bank choices when someone registers an account (Part A, step 2) or records a customer payment (Part H).
