Payments live test — Finance guide

For the Office phone — the finance seat that pays by hand and marks each payment done. This phone has been set up by the owner as the finance phone; nobody else can tap ✔ Mark Done or ✖ Decline.
Date: 09-Sep-2026  ·  Telegram: Black Panther_Bot  ·  Nothing to do until both admins have signed
The one payment everyone is testing

Mr. Abdul (Operations Officer) asks for ₦4,000 into OPAY 7048940378, reason Transport to Idumota, with a photo of the bill.

The Owner signs first, Admin 2 signs second. A payment needs two different admins when two are set up; if the Owner is the only admin, his one approval completes it. The person who asked can never sign his own.

The Office phone (finance) pays by hand in the bank app, taps ✔ Mark Done, then sends the screenshot.

In this guide the finance seat is called Office — that is the name the bot prints on the Paid notice.

Before you start
  • The owner has set this phone up as the finance phone and restarted the bot. Without that the finance card goes to the admins, not to you.
  • This phone is an allowed bot user (an active row on the Users list). If the bot answers You are not authorized to use this bot. to anything, stop and tell the owner.
  • You have sent /start to the bot.
  • Open 💳 Payments once now: the button 💳 Waiting for me to pay (0) must be there. It only shows for the finance seat — that is how you know the seat is set (step 1).
  • The bank app is on this phone and ready — the payment itself is made outside the bot, by hand.
  • Have a way to take a screenshot of the transfer; the first test payment sends the proof, the second skips it.
  • This phone need not be an admin. If it is, it will also receive the 🔔 approval cards — ignore those; they are the admins' job.

B
✖ Decline — you refuse to pay an approved request
Do: on a fresh request that both admins have signed (the owner will tell you which), tap ✖ Decline on its finance card, then type a reason of at least 3 characters — for the test: Account name does not match — send the OPAY name.
You receive: the ✖ Decline ₦4,000 to Abdul prompt; after your reason, a new ✖ Declined card arrives below with your reason in grey and The requester has been told. The prompt stays on screen — its ❌ Cancel button now only prints ❌ Cancelled. and undoes nothing (the decline has already happened). Every copy of the finance card loses its buttons.
If the bot says something else:
  • ⚠️ Give a reason — the requester needs to know what to fix. — reason too short; type it again.
  • ❌ Cancelled. after ❌ Cancel — nothing changed; the payment stays approved and waiting.
  • alert Only the finance person can decline a payment.
  • alert That payment was not found.
  • alert That payment is done — nothing to decline.
