🚚 Sending bales to another store

How to raise a transfer request in the bot, with the bale numbers you want sent.
For office staff. Anyone with bot access can raise one. Example used throughout: 3 bales of 9006 · Shade 3 from Lagos to Kano office, numbers 869, 843 and 874. Abdul sends, Musa receives.
THE ONE RULE
You type the bale numbers. The bot never chooses bales for you.
Your numbers are shown to Abdul as 📌 Ordered when he packs the load. If one of your bales is gone, the card tells you, and a person decides what replaces it.
Two ways to do it

A · Type one line — Transfer 869, 843, 874 to Kano office. Fastest when you have the numbers. Works even if the bot's AI is down.

B · Tap through — 🔁 Move Stock → 🚚 Transfer Stock → store → design → shade → how many → where to.

Before you start
  • Have the bale numbers ready.
  • All bales of one request must be in the same store. Two stores = two requests.
  • Store names as the bot spells them: Lagos, IDUMOTA, Kano office.

Way A — type the bale numbers

One message: the word Transfer, the bale numbers, then to and the store.

1
Type the line
Transfer (or Move) must be the first word. Separate the numbers with commas or spaces. The words package / bale are optional. Up to 50 numbers.
Do not type the store the bales are in — the bot knows. If some of the numbers sit in another store, the card lists them with start a separate transfer.

Quick answers
  • A bale is missing from the card — read the reason next to it. Fix the number, or raise a second request for the other store.
  • Abdul packed different bales — allowed. What he packs is what moves.
  • Only some bales arrived — Musa ticks the ones that came. The rest stay on the road until they arrive.
  • I want to cancel — there is no cancel button after Send. Ask Abdul to tap ❌ Decline.
  • The bot says "This load is already open" — the same request already exists. Open that one instead of sending again.
  • The bot says "Need more info" — your line did not start with Transfer / Move, or had no number. Retype it: Transfer 832, 1186, 786 to kano.
