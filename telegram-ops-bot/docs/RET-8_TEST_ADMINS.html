The rules, in plain words

  • A rate that is already on the sale is always used. The rate you type is used only for the thans that have none.
  • Once saved, the rate cannot be changed. To change it: ❌ Reject, and Abdul raises the return again.
  • No credit happens only when an admin taps “⏭ No credit for these”. Never by itself.
  • The employee never sees the rate. He sees the credit amount.
  • You cannot type the rate on your own return. A second admin must.
  • A return that already had one approval before 20-Nov-2026 finishes the old way: no credit for thans with no rate.
  • The rate card stays open 30 minutes, time to look the rate up. After that, tap ✏️ Type the rate again.
  • Each approval is given for the rate on the check card. If the rate changed in between, nothing is approved.
